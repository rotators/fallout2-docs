# List of Fallout 1 & 2 Mods

Expanded 2026-09-12 after reviewing project repositories, NMA resource/author pages, and release listings. Descriptions and availability are based on those sources, not installation testing. This is a discovery directory, not a list of mods to install together: follow each project's requirements and compatibility notes.

Olympus 2207 replaces the setting, story, and graphics with a separate post-apocalyptic world. The linked mirror offers an English release; its uploader is not the original developer. Fallout Yesterday recreates the canceled Van Buren concept in the Fallout 2 engine and remains a playable work in progress, rather than a completed campaign. See its [downloads and walkthrough](https://www.moddb.com/mods/f3-van-buren/downloads) for the scope of the available build.

## Finding more projects

- [NMA Fallout General Modding](https://www.nma-fallout.com/forums/fallout-general-modding.18/) includes author announcements and niche add-ons that may not have separate resource listings.
- [NMA Fallout 1 resources](https://www.nma-fallout.com/resources/categories/fallout-1.2/) and [Fallout 2 resources](https://www.nma-fallout.com/resources/categories/fallout-2.5/) provide downloads and update histories.
- [ModDB Fallout 2 projects](https://www.moddb.com/games/fallout-2/mods) includes campaigns and playable development releases. Check whether a page belongs to the developer or a mirror uploader.

When adding an entry, identify the game/runtime, what the project changes, release or demo availability, dependencies, and an author/project link. Engine reimplementations and modding frameworks belong under the [main engine/project index](index.html); editors and converters belong in [Tools](tools.html).
